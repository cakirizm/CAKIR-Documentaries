# The Man Who Lived in an Airport for 18 Years — Sources

CAKIR documentary · research file. Source IDs (S#) are referenced per chapter in
`episode.json` and in `CAKIR_Airport_Script.txt`.

"Read in full" means the article text was retrieved and checked against the
script during production (12 Nov 2026 session). The others were checked
through search-engine excerpts and are used only for points that agree with
the sources read in full.

## What the film deliberately does NOT claim

- That he was physically imprisoned in a sealed transit zone (the film corrects this myth).
- Any dialogue other than the two attributed, published quotes above.
- Any diagnosis of his mental health.
- That modern footage shows him or the events of 1988–2006. Every modern clip is
  captioned with its year and location, or labelled "illustrative".
