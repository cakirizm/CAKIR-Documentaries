# CAKIR 002 — 438 Days Lost at Sea — Sources & Claims

Status key: **V** = verified in two or more independent sources · **R** = reported (single source or
Alvarenga's own account) · **D** = disputed / unverifiable, framed as such in narration.

## Deliberately omitted
- The 2015 lawsuit by Córdoba's family (alleging cannibalism). Denied by Alvarenga, no evidence was
  presented, and repeating it would amplify an unproven allegation against a living person.
