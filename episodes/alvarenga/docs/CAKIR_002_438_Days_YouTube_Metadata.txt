DESCRIPTION
In November 2012, fisherman José Salvador Alvarenga left the coast of Chiapas, Mexico, for what should have been a 30-hour trip. A storm disabled his small open boat — and the Pacific carried him west for more than a year. On January 30, 2014, he came ashore on Ebon Atoll in the Marshall Islands, some 10,000 km away.

This documentary separates what can be checked from what rests on his word alone: the official missing-boat report, the University of Hawaiʻi ocean-current model, the doctors who examined him, and the questions that remain.

Footage of the open sea, boats and wildlife is illustrative and does not show Alvarenga unless stated. Animated scenes are labelled reconstructions. The drift map is based on ocean-current modelling, not a GPS track.
