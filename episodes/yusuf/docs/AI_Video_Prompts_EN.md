# "How 7 Cows Changed a Nation's Future" — AI video prompts (per scene)

Use these with a text/image-to-video model (e.g. Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora-class tools) to produce a
photoreal version of each shot. Durations follow the rendered film's timeline

## Consistency bible (paste into every prompt or use as a style reference)

- **Look:** realistic cinematic 3D / live-action hybrid, ancient Egypt (Middle Kingdom feel), matte
  natural materials — sandstone, mud brick, linen, leather, wheat, cracked earth, water. Warm amber
  and ochre palette; nights in deep blue moonlight; dream scenes in soft desaturated mist. Gentle film
  grain, no neon, no lens flares, no glowing particles, no text in the image.
- **Camera:** slow dolly, slow orbit, slow crane, eye-level tracking. No fast whip pans, no shaky cam.
- **The ruler (al-Malik):** Egyptian ruler in an indigo linen robe with a gold collar and gold
  headdress; always seen from the side, from behind or in shadow — the face is not featured.
- **Hz. Yusuf:** NEVER show his face. Only from behind, as a silhouette against light, or his hands.
  Off-white linen robe, simple head cloth.
- **The cupbearer (freed man):** sand-coloured robe, white head cloth, middle-aged.
- **Advisors:** six men in cream and beige linen robes, some with white head cloths.
- **Cows:** realistic cattle. Fat cows: glossy brown with white patches, heavy and healthy.
  Thin cows: grey-brown, visible ribs and hip bones, slow — but never grotesque, no blood.
- **Wheat:** emmer/einkorn-like ears with awns; green ears fresh and upright, dry ears brown,
  shrivelled, drooping.
- **Negative prompt (all scenes):** blood, gore, horror, violence, monsters, faces of prophets,
  modern objects, text, logos, watermarks, neon, lens flare, cartoon style, anime, extra limbs,
  wrong number of animals.
